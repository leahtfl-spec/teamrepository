# Roles & Titles

**Inner Board is a lean team.** Every role owns an *outcome* and a *number*, not just a list of tasks. Each role also includes an explicit invitation to step up, and a list of things to stop doing.

```
                    Founder — Voice of Inner Board
                 (teaching, podcast, videos, masterclasses)
                                 │
              Abra — Chief of Staff & Head of Growth
                (owns the 1,000 number and the scorecard)
                    ┌────────────┴────────────┐
   Michelle — COO & Head of             Clint — Creative Director
     Member Experience                (brand, content, podcast production,
 (delivery, community, retention)        merch design)
           │
     Logan — App Lead
```

| Person | Title | Owns the outcome | Their number |
|--------|-------|------------------|--------------|
| Founder | Founder & Voice of Inner Board | The teaching and the voice | Hours recorded per week (protected) |
| Abra | Chief of Staff & Head of Growth | Getting to 1,000 members | New paid members per week |
| Michelle | COO & Head of Member Experience | Members who stay and practice | 30-day active rate |
| Clint | Creative Director | How the brand looks and sounds everywhere | Content shipped per week → group joins |
| Logan | App Lead | The app works, and everything is in it | Assets live on time; app issues open |

---

## Founder — Founder & Voice of Inner Board

**Mission:** Teach. Be the voice. Everything else is someone else's job.

**Owns**
- The teaching: the practice, masterclasses and the podcast's point of view.
- **One weekly Recording Block** (about half a day; Clint directs it):
  - 1–2 *Becoming You* podcast episodes
  - 7 *Rep of the Day* videos (one week's worth, 30–60 seconds each)
  - Any promotional or offer videos
- One monthly masterclass.
- One short live session per week in the community group (optional but powerful).

**Stop doing:** editing, posting, chasing files, answering logistics questions, and approving routine content.

---

## Abra — Chief of Staff & Head of Growth

**Mission:** Own the number. Get Inner Board to 1,000 members and protect the founder's time while doing it.

**Owns**
- **The scorecard.** Updates it every Friday and runs the Monday leadership meeting from it.
- **Offers and promotions:** the Founding 1,000 annual + free hat offer, Gift the Practice, and referrals. Abra sets the terms, budget and dates.
- **Podcast growth:** books guests for *Becoming You*, and pitches the founder as a guest on other podcasts (the biggest reach lever we have).
- **The email list:** we don't own a Facebook group's audience, but we own our email list.
- **Partnerships:** collaborations, affiliates, and anyone who can bring the practice to a group of people at once.
- **The founder's calendar:** guards the Recording Block.

**Invitation to step up:** Become the person who runs the *business* of Inner Board so the founder can stay focused on teaching. Be the public face for partners and podcast hosts. Abra's job moves from checking that people are on task to owning the growth number.

**Stop doing:** general check-ins. The scorecard shows who's on track.

---

## Michelle — COO & Head of Member Experience

**Mission:** Everyone who joins gets everything they paid for, builds a daily rep habit, and stays.

**Owns**
- **Masterclass production:** runs the [pipeline](../workflows/masterclass-pipeline.md) and gets the recording and every asset to Logan.
- **Membership assets:** replay, workbook, audio drops, slides — complete and live in the app. See [Membership Assets](../workflows/membership-assets.md).
- **Onboarding:** a new member's first 7 days (welcome message, first rep, how to use the app, an invitation to the group).
- **The community:** the [Rep of the Day group](../playbooks/community-rep-of-the-day.md). Welcomes new people, keeps the conversation going, moderates, and spotlights members.
- **Merch fulfillment:** collects addresses, ships the hats, handles issues.
- **Member support:** questions about access, billing or content.
- **Logan:** sets his priorities and checks his work.

**Invitation to step up:** Become the member-facing voice next to the founder. Host the monthly **New Member Welcome** call and a monthly **Member Spotlight** segment on the podcast. Be the person members know by name. Mentor Clint, rather than approving his work.

**Stop doing:** approving routine posts and writing status reports. The scorecard replaces both.

---

## Clint — Creative Director

**Mission:** Make Inner Board look and sound unmistakable everywhere, and turn the founder's weekly recording into a week of content.

**Owns**
- **Brand guidelines:** a one-page guide covering colors, fonts, logo use, caption style and voice. Once it exists, Clint publishes routine content without approval.
- **The Recording Block:** plans the shot list, sets up, and directs the founder.
- **Podcast production:** edits, publishes, cuts 3–5 clips per episode, and uploads to YouTube.
- **Rep of the Day:** produces and schedules the daily posts **2 weeks ahead**.
- **Social:** clips, carousels, posting and engagement on our pages.
- **Merch:** designs the hat and sweatshirt, runs photo and video shoots of the team and members wearing the gear.

**Invitation to step up:** Clint owns the creative calls: what gets clipped, how it looks, what we try next. He can host a short behind-the-scenes series ("how we make Inner Board"). He's a director, not an editor.

**Stop doing:** waiting for approval on routine posts.

---

## Logan — App Lead

**Mission:** The app is where the practice lives. It works, and everything is in it on time.

**Owns**
- Uploads of replays, audio drops, workbooks and Rep of the Day videos.
- App onboarding screens and the first-7-days flow, built with Michelle.
- **Gift codes and annual plans:** make sure the app or payment system can sell a gifted year and flag annual signups for the free hat.
- App numbers each week for the scorecard: active members, signups, annual vs. monthly.

## Decision rights (who decides; no approval queues)

| Decision | Who decides | Who's informed |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Routine posts, clips, Rep of the Day | Clint (within brand guidelines) | Michelle spot-checks weekly |
| Teaching content and claims | Founder | — |
| Offers, pricing, promotions, merch budget | Abra | Founder |
| Masterclass dates and topics | Founder + Abra | Michelle |
| Member issues, refunds under $X, fulfillment | Michelle | Abra |
| App releases and uploads | Logan | Michelle |
