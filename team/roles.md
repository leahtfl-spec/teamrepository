# Roles & Responsibilities

Each person has a clear lane. When something falls between lanes, **Michelle decides**; when it's a priority call or a blocker Michelle can't clear, **Abra decides**.

---

## Abra — Oversight / Lead

**Purpose:** Make sure everyone is on task and the business is moving in the right direction.

**Responsibilities**
- Run the **weekly review** (see [`schedule/weekly-cadence.md`](../schedule/weekly-cadence.md)): what shipped, what's late, what's blocked.
- Set priorities for the month (which masterclasses, launches, campaigns).
- Final approval on: launch announcements, pricing/offer posts, anything sensitive or brand-defining.
- Unblock Michelle when something needs a decision above operations.
- Hold Michelle accountable; Michelle holds Clint and Logan accountable.

**Reports to Abra:** Michelle (directly), Clint and Logan (through Michelle).

---

## Michelle — COO

**Purpose:** Run day-to-day operations, train and lead the team, and own every masterclass from recording to delivery.

**Responsibilities — Team**
- Train and lead **Clint**: content standards, brand voice, posting schedule, approval of clips before they go out.
- Oversee **Logan**: make sure every masterclass recording and membership asset gets to the app, on time and correct.
- Keep the content calendar filled at least **2 weeks ahead**.
- Report status to Abra every week.

**Responsibilities — Masterclass & Membership Assets**
Michelle is the **owner** of each masterclass and its assets. That means:

| Stage | Michelle's job |
|-------|----------------|
| Before | Confirm date, topic, and recording setup; make sure Clint is scheduled to capture; prep the workbook outline. |
| During | Make sure the recording is actually running (and has a backup). |
| After (within 24h) | Collect the raw recording and drop it in the shared folder; notify Clint (for clips) and Logan (for the app). |
| Assets | Make sure the **workbook, audio drop, replay, slides/resources** are finished and handed to Logan. |
| Delivery | Verify in the app that everything is live and works. Tell Abra it's done. |

Full step-by-step: [`workflows/masterclass-pipeline.md`](../workflows/masterclass-pipeline.md)

**Approves:** Clint's clips and posts (unless Abra approval is required), Logan's uploads (final check in the app).

---

## Clint — Marketing / Video

**Purpose:** Turn our content into posts that look great and get people engaged.

**Responsibilities**
- Capture video at masterclasses and content shoots.
- Clip and edit: short-form clips (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), highlight reels, quote graphics.
- Make everything on-brand ("pretty"): captions, thumbnails, branding, music.
- Post according to the [content calendar](../schedule/content-calendar.csv).
- Handle day-to-day engagement per [`playbooks/how-to-engage.md`](../playbooks/how-to-engage.md).
- Send clips to Michelle for approval **before** posting.

**Reports to:** Michelle.

---

## Logan — App

**Purpose:** Make sure members find everything they paid for, in the app, working.

**Responsibilities**
- Upload each masterclass **replay** to the app within the agreed window (see pipeline).
- Upload all **membership assets**: workbook, audio drops, slides/resources.
- Make sure titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and ordering are correct.
- Test that everything plays/downloads on phone and desktop.
- Report anything broken or missing to Michelle immediately.

**Reports to:** Michelle.
