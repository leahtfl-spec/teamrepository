## What going big requires

**Stage 1: 1,000 practicing each week by Nov 9, 2027.** The first milestone, 250 by Mar 31, is about 20 weeks away.

- **Must be true:** about 65 people start Practice each week, assuming 1 in 3 keep going. Every episode, clip and room is one tap from a first rep. There is one clear count. Michelle has time for retention, and Leah's hours are protected.
- **Check the math.** The goal shows 0, but that's an empty field. Skool and premium members already practicing may count, so pull the baselines (due Nov 13) before trusting 65. On the other side, Michelle's target is 40% still practicing at day 30. If more drop off later, fewer than 1 in 3 stay, and 65 is a floor (our inference). [Road to 1,000](#/library/road-to-1000)'s funnel is still built for the old paid goal (about 1,050 group members a month); the new target is about 280 starters a month.
- **We have:** a content engine, the podcast, the group, two well-timed campaigns, a retention-first scorecard, and coaches and leaders meeting Abra weekly.
- **Missing:** a new-starters owner, a definition of practicing, baselines (all 22 blank), proof the app can do gift codes, the annual marker and welcome-back, tracking of where people come from (due Nov 30), and people to do the work.
- **Start now:** count practice through existing INQ coaches and IOS leaders, and give each a "Start Practice" link with its own tag, as an extension of Abra's room kit. It's the first step of "the many teaching many", and it counts toward 1,000 this year.

## What not to do yet

- **No paid ads** until there are prices, a budget and a way to see where people come from.
- **No new offers, names or platforms** beyond what's decided (the Facebook group, Gift a Year, Founding 1,000) until the brand map is in. Abra's programs for her rooms, built on the existing offer, can go ahead.
- **No cold push to new organizations** without a one-pager, a price and results. Working with current IOS leaders starts now.
- **No promises of automatic streaks or rewards** until Logan confirms the app can do them.
- **No bulk hat order without the numbers.** If they aren't in by Nov 20, run "hat ships in January", as the plan allows.
- **Don't add the new-starters number to anyone's list** without taking something off.
