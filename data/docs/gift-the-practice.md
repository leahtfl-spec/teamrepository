# Gift the Practice

Our gear does three jobs:

1. **A reason to commit now:** a free hat with an annual plan.
2. **Recognition:** the team wears it on camera, and members wear it in their photos.
3. **Belonging:** a member in an InnerBoard hat is a walking invitation.

**Offer:** Abra. **Design:** Clint. **Fulfillment:** Michelle. **Gift codes and annual flag:** Logan.

## The three programs

**1. Founding 1,000: join annually, get a free hat**
- Anyone who buys or upgrades to an **annual** plan gets an InnerBoard hat.
- Framed as **Founding Member**, limited to the first 1,000 annual members.
- Fine print to settle: one per member, shipping region (US only?), ship time, while supplies last.

**2. Gift a Year (holiday season)**
- Buy a year of the practice for someone; they receive a welcome hat and a card.
- Needs gift codes in the app or payment system. Logan confirms this in Phase 0.

**3. Streak rewards**
- 100 Rep of the Day reps earns an InnerBoard sweatshirt.
- Tracked by hand at first (members post "Day 100").

## The team wears it

- Leah: in every video, podcast recording and live session.
- Abra, Michelle and Clint: in their own on-camera moments and team content.
- Clint runs one team shoot in the gear. That content feeds December and January.

## Check the economics first (Abra)

| Item | Fill in |
|------|---------|
| Annual price | $ |
| Hat cost (unit + embroidery) | $ |
| Shipping and packaging per hat | $ |
| **Total cost per hat** | $ |
| **Hat as a share of the annual price** | aim for under about 15% |

**Rule:** if the hat costs more than about 15% of the annual price, raise the price, find a cheaper vendor, or limit the offer to the first N members.

## Sourcing

| Option | Good | Not so good | Use for |
|--------|------|-------------|---------|
| **Bulk order** (local embroiderer or bulk vendor) | Cheapest per unit; hats on hand for gifts and shoots | Cash up front; we hold inventory | The team and the first wave (start with about 100–150) |
| **Print-on-demand** (made per order) | No inventory, no upfront cost | Higher unit cost, slower, less quality control | Sweatshirts, and a store where members buy their own |

> **Timing:** embroidered hats take **2–4 weeks**. Order by about **November 20** to have them for December.

## Fulfillment (Michelle)

1. Logan flags new annual and gift sign-ups each week.
2. Michelle sends a short address form.
3. Hats ship in one weekly batch.
4. Hats owed and hats shipped are counted in the weekly shipping task on [Tasks](#/tasks).
5. Members post a photo in the hat, and Clint reposts it.
