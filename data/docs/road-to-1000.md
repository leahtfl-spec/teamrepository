# Road to 1,000

**The goal:** 1,000 paying members practicing in the app.
**Owner:** Abra, Chief of Staff & Head of Growth.

> Every number on this page is a **starting assumption**. We replace each one with real data as soon as we have it. Track the real numbers on [Numbers](#/numbers).

## The date

The plan assumes **12 months, to November 30, 2027**. That is about **84 new members a month, or about 20 a week**. A shorter deadline means a bigger weekly number. The Founder and Abra confirm the date in Phase 0.

## How people find us and stay

| Step | What it is | Where |
|------|------------|-------|
| 1. Attention | The *Becoming You* podcast, the founder on other podcasts, clips, the team in the gear | Podcast, social |
| 2. Front door (free) | The Rep of the Day group: a daily practice, with email captured at the door | Facebook group |
| 3. Commitment (paid) | The app: annual plan with a free hat, or monthly. The monthly masterclass invitation converts | App |
| 4. Stay | First-7-days onboarding, daily reps, the monthly masterclass, spotlights and streaks | App + group |
| 5. Loop | Members gift a year or bring a friend, and a new person enters at step 1 | Everywhere |

Every piece of content has **one job**: move someone one step forward.

## The funnel math

| Step | Assumption | Per month to reach about 84 new paid |
|------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| New free group members | — | **about 1,050** |
| Group → masterclass registrants | 40% | about 420 |
| Registrants who show up | 50% | about 210 |
| Attendees who join the app | 25% | about 53 |
| Direct joins (podcast, social, referrals, gifts) | — | about 31 |
| **Total new paid** | | **about 84** |

**What the math tells us:** the real constraint is **about 1,000 new people a month entering the free group** (about 250 a week). They come from:

- **The podcast**, with one call to action every episode: "Join the free Rep of the Day group."
- **The founder on other podcasts**: 2–4 guest spots a month is the highest-leverage reach for a lean team.
- **Clips** that send people to the group, not just likes.
- **Members inviting people**: gift a year, and "bring a friend to the 30-Day Rep Challenge."

**First action (Abra, this week):** write down where we are right now: paid members, annual vs. monthly, email list size, podcast downloads per episode, social followers. You can't manage a gap you haven't measured.

## The rollout

The plan is built around the calendar we are in: **gift season (December) and New Year (January)** are the two best moments of the year for a practice called *Becoming You*.

| Phase | When | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| 0 · Decide | Week of Nov 9 | Roles, target date, baseline numbers, free group, annual price, gift codes, hat samples |
| 1 · Foundation | Nov 16–30 | First Recording Block, brand guidelines, launch the group, onboarding, bulk hat order, podcast call to action, guest pitches |
| 2 · Gift the Practice | Dec 1–24 | Gift a Year, weekly founder live session, team in the gear, December masterclass, weekly hat shipping |
| 3 · Founding 1,000 | Dec 26 – Jan 31 | Founding 1,000 offer, 30-Day Rep Challenge from Jan 1, January masterclass, guest appearances begin |
| 4 · Scale | February on | Masterclass engine runs itself, referrals, streak rewards, Member Spotlight, retention check, quarterly review |

> **Timing:** custom embroidered hats usually take **2–4 weeks** to produce and ship. To have hats for December, **order by about November 20**. If that slips, run Gift the Practice as "hat ships in January" and say so up front.
