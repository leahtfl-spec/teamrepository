# Membership assets

Everything a member gets, who makes it, and who puts it in the **Practice app**. The Practice app is free to use daily, with premium features for members. Members hear about new things in the app and by email through **GHL**.

## The monthly drop

Each month the **recording, workbook, audio note and surprise drop** reach members together. Michelle owns it, with Logan. The step-by-step checklist is the [Monthly drop](#/library/monthly-drop).

## Every asset

| Asset | What it is | Made by | Uploaded by | Due |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------------|-----|
| Recording (Masterclass replay) | The full, cleaned recording | Clint | Logan | Day +3 |
| Workbook | PDF exercises and notes for the Masterclass | Michelle | Logan | Day +3 |
| Audio note | A short listen for the month (monthly drop) | To confirm | Logan | Drop day |
| Surprise drop | One extra members don't expect (monthly drop) | Michelle | Logan | Drop day |
| Audio drop | Audio-only version of the recording, for listening on the go | Clint | Logan | Day +3 |
| Slides and resources | Slide deck, links and templates from the class | Michelle | Logan | Day +3 |
| Bonus audio drops | Stand-alone audio between Masterclasses | Michelle (content), Clint (production) | Logan | Per the calendar |
| Highlight clips | Short "best of" clips for members | Clint | Logan | Day +7 |
| Rep of the Day videos | Daily practice reps | Leah (record), Clint (edit) | Logan | Weekly |
| Podcast episodes | *Becoming You* episodes as audio drops | Clint | Logan | Weekly |

Days count from the Masterclass (Day 0). See the [Masterclass pipeline](#/library/masterclass-pipeline).

## Logan's app checklist

For every asset:

- The title matches the Masterclass or drop title exactly
- A short description (1–2 sentences)
- A thumbnail or cover image
- In the right section, in the right order
- Plays or downloads on a **phone**
- Plays or downloads on a **desktop**
- Michelle told it's in
