# Membership assets

Everything a member gets, who makes it, and who puts it in the app.

| Asset | What it is | Made by | Uploaded by | Due |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------------|-----|
| Masterclass replay | The full, cleaned recording | Clint | Logan | Day +3 |
| Audio drop | Audio-only version for listening on the go | Clint | Logan | Day +3 |
| Workbook | PDF exercises and notes for the masterclass | Michelle | Logan | Day +3 |
| Slides and resources | Slide deck, links and templates from the class | Michelle | Logan | Day +3 |
| Bonus audio drops | Stand-alone audio between masterclasses | Michelle (content), Clint (production) | Logan | Per the calendar |
| Highlight clips | Short "best of" clips for members | Clint | Logan | Day +7 |
| Rep of the Day videos | Daily practice reps, also posted in the group | Founder (record), Clint (edit) | Logan | Weekly |
| Podcast episodes | *Becoming You* episodes as audio drops | Clint | Logan | Weekly |

Days count from the masterclass (Day 0). See the [Masterclass pipeline](#/library/masterclass-pipeline).

## Logan's app checklist

For every asset:

- The title matches the masterclass title exactly
- A short description (1–2 sentences)
- A thumbnail or cover image
- In the right section, in the right order
- Plays or downloads on a **phone**
- Plays or downloads on a **desktop**
- Michelle told it's in
