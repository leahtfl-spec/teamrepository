# Go-to-market gaps

*Nov 10, 2026. Read against all three win sheets: [Abra](#/library/abra-role), [Michelle](#/library/michelle-win-sheet) and [Clint](#/library/clint-win-sheet).*

**The short version:** the team is strong inside the cell. Michelle owns the member experience, Clint owns content and social, and Abra owns the brand and the rooms. What's missing is clear ownership of the moment a stranger becomes someone who **starts Practice**, and a simple map of what we offer once they arrive.

## The math we're up against

Michelle's goal is 1,000 people practicing each week by Nov 9, 2027. Suppose about 1 in 3 people who start Practice become weekly practicers. That's an assumption; Michelle's day-30 baseline will replace it. On that assumption, about **3,000 people need to start Practice in the next 12 months**. That's **60–70 new people every week**.

Clint's numbers stop at reach, engagement and link clicks. Michelle's numbers start once someone is a member. **No one owns the step in between**, so it's on the scorecard now as "New people starting Practice each week", owner to be decided.

## Where the Rep of the Day group fits

**Decided Nov 10: we're doing it.** The Facebook group is the free, public, fun front door, where people do one rep a day together. Each daily post links into the **Practice app**, where streaks, the Labs and premium live. The group brings people in and the app keeps them. The scorecard counts both: group members, and new people starting Practice. See [Rep of the Day](#/library/rep-of-the-day).
