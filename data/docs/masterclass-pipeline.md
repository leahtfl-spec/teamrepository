# Masterclass pipeline

How one masterclass becomes a replay in the app, a full set of member assets, and two weeks of content.

**Michelle owns the whole pipeline.** Every step has one person and one date. To start one, add the steps below as tasks on [Tasks](#/tasks) with real dates.

## Timeline

Day 0 is the day of the masterclass.

| When | Step | Owner | Hands off to |
|------|------|-------|-----------|
| Day −7 | Confirm date, topic, title and promo plan | Michelle | Founder + Abra (approve), Clint |
| Day −7 | Draft the workbook outline | Michelle | — |
| Day −7 to −1 | Masterclass invites by email (GHL) and in the Practice app | Michelle | — |
| Day −5 to −1 | Promo posts go out: announcement, reminder, "tomorrow," "today" | Clint | — |
| Day −1 | Tech check: recording software, audio, backup recording | Michelle + Clint | — |
| **Day 0** | **Run the masterclass and record it (with a backup)** | Michelle | — |
| Day 0 | Capture behind-the-scenes footage, team in the gear | Clint | — |
| Day +1 | Raw recording in the shared folder; Clint and Logan told | Michelle | Clint, Logan |
| Day +2 | Clean replay edit (trim, fix audio) | Clint | Logan |
| Day +2 | Audio drop exported | Clint | Logan |
| Day +3 | Workbook finalized | Michelle | Logan |
| Day +3 | Replay, audio drop, workbook and slides **live in the app** | Logan | Michelle |
| Day +3 | Check the app on phone and desktop, sign off, announce to members (Practice app + GHL) | Michelle | The team |
| Day +3 to +14 | Clips posted; each one sends people to the group or the app | Clint | — |

Adjust the days to what works. The point is that **every step has a date and a name**.

## Shared folder

Every masterclass gets its own folder, named by date and title (for example *Masterclasses / 2026-11-15 – Title*), with the same six folders inside, so nobody has to ask where something is:

| Folder | What goes in it |
|--------|-----------------|
| `01-raw` | Michelle's raw recording |
| `02-replay` | Clint's cleaned replay |
| `03-audio` | The audio drop (mp3) |
| `04-workbook` | The workbook (PDF) |
| `05-slides` | Slides and extra resources |
| `06-clips` | Clint's short clips and graphics |

## Handoff rules

1. **Every handoff is announced.** Mark the task done and tell the next person ("Logan, the replay is in 02-replay").
2. **Nothing goes in the app until Michelle has signed off** on the file.
3. **Clint publishes clips on his own**, within the brand guidelines. Only offer and pricing posts need Abra's sign-off.
4. **If a step will be late, say so before the due date.**

## Content from every masterclass

- 5–8 short clips (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- 2–3 quote graphics and 1 carousel of the key lessons
- 1 "Missed it? It's in the membership" post
- 1 highlight reel
