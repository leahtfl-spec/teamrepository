# The Becoming You podcast

**The podcast is how people find us.** Every episode gives real value and ends with **one** call to action.

**Host:** Leah. **Producer:** Clint. **Growth and guests:** Abra. **Member Spotlight:** Michelle.

## Cadence

- One episode a **week**, released the same day each week (Thursday).
- Recorded in Leah's weekly Recording Block, **2 weeks ahead**.

## Every episode includes

- One clear call to action, rotated by phase:
  - Default: "Join the free Rep of the Day group"
  - December: "Gift the practice to someone you love"
  - January: "Join the Founding 1,000 and get your hat"
- Leah wearing the gear, if the episode is filmed.
- 3–5 short clips cut for social (Clint).
- The full episode on YouTube (Clint).
- The audio in the app as a member audio drop, where it fits (Logan).
- Downloads recorded on [Numbers](#/numbers) (Clint).

## Episode types

| Type | How often | Who |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Leah solo teaching | Twice a month | Leah |
| Guest conversation | Once a month | Leah + a guest booked by Abra |
| **Member Spotlight:** a member's story of becoming | Once a month | Michelle hosts or co-hosts |
| Behind InnerBoard: the team, the gear, how we build | Quarterly | Leah + team |

## Growth (Abra)

- Pitch Leah to **2–4 other podcasts a month**. This is our biggest reach lever.
- Trade guest spots with similar shows.
- Ask listeners to share one episode with one friend.
