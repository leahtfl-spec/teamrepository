# What to post

Every post fits one of five pillars. Aim for a mix across the week. The Rep of the Day runs separately in the group; see [Rep of the Day](#/library/rep-of-the-day). Plan posts on [Content](#/content).

## Content pillars

The percentages are starting assumptions. Adjust them to what performs.

| Pillar | Goal | Examples | Share of posts |
|--------|------|----------|----------------|
| **Masterclass clips** | Show the value of what we teach | 30–60 second teaching moments, "aha" quotes | about 30% |
| **Education** | Give value for free, build trust | Tips, carousels, myths vs. facts | about 25% |
| **Community & wins** | Social proof | Member stories, results, shout-outs | about 15% |
| **Behind the scenes** | Make us human | The team in the gear, the Recording Block, how we build | about 15% |
| **Promo & offers** | Drive sign-ups | Masterclass invitations, the Founding 1,000 hat, Gift a Year | about 15% |

## One masterclass, two weeks of content

- 5–8 short clips (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- 2–3 quote graphics
- 1 carousel summarizing the key lessons
- 1 "if you missed it, it's in the membership" post
- 1 highlight reel

## Before anything goes out (Clint)

- A hook in the first 2 seconds
- Captions on every video
- On-brand fonts, colors and logo
- **One** clear call to action, usually "join the free Rep of the Day group"
- Leah and team in the gear where possible
- Caption and hashtags written
- On the [Content](#/content) calendar
- Offer or pricing posts only: Abra has signed off

## Post status

Idea → Planned → In edit → Scheduled → Posted. Click a status on [Content](#/content) to move it along. Offer and pricing posts get Abra's sign-off before they are scheduled.
