# Monthly drop

> **Draft for Michelle and Logan.** A starting checklist, built from Michelle's win sheet. Change anything that doesn't match how you work, then delete this line.

Every month, members get four things, together and clearly: the **recording**, the **workbook**, an **audio note**, and a **surprise drop**. **Michelle** owns the drop reaching members. **Logan** gets it live in the Practice app.

**How we know it worked:** 60% of members open or use the drop within its first week (on [Numbers](#/numbers)).

## The four pieces

| Piece | What it is | Made by | Live in the app |
|-------|------------|---------|-----------------|
| Recording | The month's Masterclass, cleaned up (see the [Masterclass pipeline](#/library/masterclass-pipeline)) | Clint edits | Logan |
| Workbook | A PDF to go with the month's theme | Michelle | Logan |
| Audio note | A short listen for the month | To confirm | Logan |
| Surprise drop | One extra members don't expect | Michelle picks it | Logan |

## Every month

| When | Step | Who |
|------|------|-----|
| First Monday | The month's theme is set at the theme talk | Leah + Abra |
| Two weeks before | Pick the drop date and the surprise; tell Logan | Michelle |
| Two weeks before | Plan the announcement on [Content](#/content) | Michelle |
| Masterclass week | Recording cleaned and in the shared folder | Clint |
| Masterclass week | Workbook and audio note final | Michelle |
| Drop day −1 | All four pieces in the app, in the right section, checked on a phone and a desktop | Logan |
| Drop day −1 | Michelle checks it and signs off | Michelle |
| Drop day | Announce it in the Practice app | Michelle (Logan for in-app notifications) |
| Drop day | Email it through GHL | Michelle |
| One week after | Open or use rate on Numbers | Michelle, with Logan's app numbers |
| One week after | One thing to change next month | Michelle + Logan |
