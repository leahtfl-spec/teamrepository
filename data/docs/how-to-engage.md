# How to engage

How we talk with the people who find us.

- **Social accounts** (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube): **Clint**.
- **The Rep of the Day group and members:** **Michelle**. See [Rep of the Day](#/library/rep-of-the-day).

## How fast we reply

| Where | Reply within |
|-------|----------------|
| Comments on our posts | Same day, ideally in the first hour after posting |
| Direct messages | 24 hours on weekdays |
| Member questions in the app or group | 24 hours; send access or content questions to Michelle |
| Complaints, refunds, tech issues | Never solve in public. Move to a direct message and bring in Michelle |

## Clint's daily routine (20–30 minutes, twice a day)

1. Reply to every comment on posts from the last 48 hours.
2. Answer direct messages (saved replies below).
3. Like or comment on 10 posts from followers or people in our space.
4. Reshare member posts and tags to Stories.
5. Save great testimonials for the Community & wins pillar.

## Our voice

- Warm, encouraging, real. Talk like a person, not a brand.
- Use their name when you can.
- Ask a question back to keep the conversation going.
- Never argue in public.

## Saved replies

Fill in the links before using these.

## Hand it to Michelle

- Anything about payment, refunds or account access
- Negative or critical comments that need a careful reply
- Press, collaboration or partnership requests
- Anything you're unsure about
