# Weekly rhythm

**The live calendar is [Team → Our week](#/team).** Each person's daily, weekly and monthly commitments live there, and it is the one to change when the week changes. This page explains how the week fits together. Posts live on [Content](#/content), numbers on [Numbers](#/numbers), and roles on [Team](#/team).

## The anchors

| Day | What | Who |
|-----|------|-----|
| **Mon** | Team meeting: direction, connection, support | Abra (chief of staff) |
| **Tue** | **Recording Block** (about half a day): podcast, 7 Reps, promo videos | Leah, directed by Clint |
| **Thu** | Rep of the Day scheduled two weeks out | Clint |
| **Fri** | Numbers updated on the scorecard | Michelle |
| **Fri** | Weekly app check | Logan |

Everything else (office hours, the newsletter, the Coaching Lab, YouTube) sits under "No set day yet" on Our week until its day is set.

## Every day

- **Rep of the Day** goes out at 6am, scheduled by Clint.
- **Michelle:** opens Practice and posts her reps; replies and welcomes in the Practice app and the Labs; member messages and issues (app issues go to Logan).

## Every month

| When | What | Who |
|------|------|-----|
| First Monday | Theme talk: the month's theme | Leah + Abra |
| Monthly | Masterclass invites, then the Masterclass (see the [pipeline](#/library/masterclass-pipeline)) | Michelle + Leah + Clint |
| Monthly | The [monthly drop](#/library/monthly-drop): recording, workbook, audio note, surprise drop | Michelle + Logan |
| By month end | Next month's social content plan approved | Michelle |
| Last Friday | Monthly review: are we on pace for the next milestone? | Michelle → Abra and Leah |

## The Monday team meeting

Abra runs it in 30 minutes, from [Numbers](#/numbers):

1. **The numbers:** people practicing each week against the next milestone (250 by Mar 31), plus Michelle's member numbers. Is each one on track?
2. **Off track:** what we change this week.
3. **This week:** Recording Block booked for Tuesday; Reps scheduled two weeks out; podcast episode and call to action ready; newsletter; next Masterclass and monthly drop on track.
4. **Decisions needed from Leah.**

## Who decides

No approval queues. Each decision has one owner.

| Decision | Who decides | Who's informed |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Routine posts, clips, Rep of the Day | Clint (within the brand guidelines) | Michelle spot-checks weekly |
| Teaching content and claims | Leah | — |
| The standard: names, shared words, how an offer is put | Abra | The team |
| Offers, pricing, promotions, merch budget | Abra | Leah |
| Masterclass dates and topics; the monthly theme | Leah + Abra | Michelle |
| Member issues, small refunds, fulfillment | Michelle | Abra |
| App releases and uploads | Logan | Michelle |
