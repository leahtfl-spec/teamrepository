# Weekly rhythm

The week is built around one thing: **the founder's Recording Block**. Everything else feeds it or shares it. Posts live on [Content](#/content), numbers on [Numbers](#/numbers), and roles on [Team](#/team).

## Every week

| Day | Founder | Abra | Michelle | Clint | Logan |
|-----|---------|------|----------|-------|-------|
| **Mon** | 15 min at the leadership meeting (optional) | **Runs the leadership meeting** (30 min, from Numbers) | Leadership meeting; group welcome post | Leadership meeting; plan the Recording Block shot list | Fix any open app issues |
| **Tue** | **Recording Block** (about half a day): podcast, 7 Reps, promo videos | Guards the founder's calendar; podcast pitches | Masterclass prep and onboarding | **Directs and shoots the Recording Block** | — |
| **Wed** | — | Guest bookings; offers and promotions | Community; member support | Edit the podcast and Reps | Upload new assets |
| **Thu** | — | Partnerships; email list | Check in with Logan; ship hats | **Podcast episode releases**; post clips; schedule Reps 2 weeks out | Upload Reps and audio to the app |
| **Fri** | **Live session in the group** (15–20 min) | **Update Numbers** | **Wins Thread** and member spotlight; group numbers to Abra | Social numbers and podcast downloads to Abra | App numbers to Abra |
| **Sat/Sun** | — | — | Light group check (15 min a day) | Scheduled posts go out | — |

## Every day

- **Rep of the Day** goes out at 6am, scheduled by Clint.
- **Michelle:** two 15-minute passes through the group: replies, welcomes, moderation.
- **Clint:** two passes through comments and messages on our social accounts.

## The Monday leadership meeting

Abra runs it in 30 minutes from [Numbers](#/numbers):

1. **The numbers:** new paid (target about 20), annual sign-ups, new group members (target about 250), avg. Rep comments, podcast downloads, hats owed and shipped. Is each one on track?
2. **Off track:** what we change this week.
3. **This week:** Recording Block booked for Tuesday; Reps scheduled 2 weeks out; podcast episode and call to action ready; group welcome and Wins Thread; hats shipped; next masterclass on track.
4. **Decisions needed from the Founder.**

## Who decides

No approval queues. Each decision has one owner.

| Decision | Who decides | Who's informed |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Routine posts, clips, Rep of the Day | Clint (within the brand guidelines) | Michelle spot-checks weekly |
| Teaching content and claims | Founder | — |
| Offers, pricing, promotions, merch budget | Abra | Founder |
| Masterclass dates and topics | Founder + Abra | Michelle |
| Member issues, small refunds, fulfillment | Michelle | Abra |
| App releases and uploads | Logan | Michelle |
