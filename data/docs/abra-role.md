# Abra · Brand and Mission Director

**Brand and Mission Director.** It sits beside Certification Director and names both halves of the work: the standard Abra holds and the mission she carries out into the world.

## The role in one line

> Abra holds the InnerBoard standard and carries it into every room she walks into.

## How the role works: the cell wall

Abra pictures InnerBoard as a cell, with Michelle, Clint, and Logan inside doing the work. A cell wall faces both directions. It decides what comes in and what goes out, and it is the part of the cell that meets the world. Holding the brand and stepping into rooms are one job, seen from two sides.

### Inside the wall: the brand

- **Owns the standard.** The Baseline, the Constitution, and the shared words are Abra's. Every name, line, and offer traces back to her.
- **Teaches the standard so it travels.** The team applies it with confidence, and Abra brings her eye to what is new.
- **Boils it down.** Every offer, page, and program reaches its simplest, most potent form before it ships.
- **Carries the doctrine.** Owns everything conceptual in the curriculum and leads INQ as Certification Director.
- **Shapes the monthly theme** with Leah at the first Monday theme talk.

### Outside the wall: the presence

- **Builds the signature talk.** What InnerBoard is, why it matters, and the invitation into the Practice, said so clearly and so bravely that it lands in any room.
- **Steps into rooms.** Groups, organizations, conferences, coaches, schools, and communities, anywhere the work belongs.
- **Creates programs.** The offer is built and it stands, so Abra builds programs on top of it for the people in those rooms.
- **Travels and speaks.** The work goes wherever her laptop goes.

## Relationships, inside and outside the cell

Abra builds relationships with InnerBoard's people on both sides of the wall.

- **Serves as chief of staff.** The team's go-to for direction, connection, and support.
- **Hosts office hours for IOS Certification leaders**, guiding them as they bring the work into their organizations.
- **Hosts INQ office hours and leads INQ conversations**, guiding coaches through certification and beyond.
- **Builds relationships** with members, clients, partners, and the people in every room she enters.

## The big dream: a TED talk

The TED talk is Abra's big, hairy dream: to become so good at what she says, and so brave about how she says it, that she steps onto that stage and gives it everything. Every talk before it is practice for that one. It just takes practice.

### Dream bigger

- Speaking and travel become one life: talks booked in the places she wants to see.
- The TED talk becomes the seed of a keynote that other stages ask for by name.
- The talk becomes something people pass on, and the many teaching many starts with the people in her rooms.

## How Abra knows it's working

- The brand holds its shape in everything the team sends out.
- Every room ends with an invitation into the Practice, INQ, or InnerBoard OS.
- The talk improves with every rep, and her voice in the room grows braver.
- She stands on a TED stage.

## Time and shape

The work happens from anywhere and flexes around travel, coaching, and speaking.
