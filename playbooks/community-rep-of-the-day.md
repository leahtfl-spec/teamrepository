# Rep of the Day: Facebook Group

**What it is:** a free Facebook group where members get one small practice rep a day, do it, and comment "done", plus a short reflection. It's the daily habit and the front door to the app.

**Owner:** Michelle (community). **Content:** Clint. **Voice:** Founder.

> **Decision:** free or members-only? **Recommendation: free.** The group is where people try the practice and the app is where they go deeper. Paid members get the full library, masterclasses, workbooks and audio drops.

## Daily format

Every day at about 6am, a scheduled post goes up:

> **Rep of the Day — [Day #]**
> [30–60 sec founder video, or a graphic with one prompt]
> *Today's rep:* [one clear action]
> 👇 Comment **"done"** + one word for how it felt.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Post |
|-----|------|
| Mon | Intention for the week + Rep |
| Tue–Thu | Rep of the Day |
| Fri | Rep + **Wins Thread** ("share one win this week") |
| Sat | Reflection rep |
| Sun | Rest + preview of next week + podcast episode link |
| Weekly | 15–20 min **founder live session** |
| Weekly | **Welcome post** tagging new members (Michelle) |

## Entry questions (to capture the email list)

1. What brought you to Inner Board?
2. What do you want to become this year?
3. What's your email? We'll send you the free [lead magnet] and the 30-Day Rep Challenge. (Make it optional and say what you'll send.)

## Group rules (short version)

1. Do the rep, then share.
2. Encourage others; don't advise unless asked.
3. No selling or self-promotion.
4. What's shared here stays here.

## Streaks and recognition

- Shout-outs at 7, 30 and 100 reps (tracked manually at first: members comment "Day 30").
- A 100-rep streak earns an Inner Board sweatshirt. See [Gift the Practice](gift-the-practice.md).
