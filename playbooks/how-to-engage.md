# How to Engage

How we interact with our audience.

- **Social accounts** (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube): **Clint**.
- **Rep of the Day group and members**: **Michelle**. See [Rep of the Day](community-rep-of-the-day.md).

## Response times

| Where | Respond within |
|-------|----------------|
| Comments on our posts | Same day (aim for first hour after posting) |
| DMs | 24 hours (weekdays) |
| Member questions in the app/community | 24 hours — route to Michelle if it's about access or content |
| Complaints / refunds / tech issues | Don't solve publicly — move to DM, tag Michelle |

## Daily engagement routine (Clint, ~20–30 min, twice a day)

1. Reply to every comment on posts from the last 48 hours.
2. Answer DMs (use saved replies below).
3. Like/comment on 10 posts from followers or people in our niche.
4. Reshare any member posts/tags to Stories.
5. Note any great testimonials → add to the "Community & wins" pillar.

## Voice

- Warm, encouraging, real. Talk like a person, not a brand.
- Use their name when you can.
- Ask a question back to keep the conversation going.
- Never argue in public.

## Saved replies (customize)

- **"How do I join?"** → "So glad you asked! 💛 Everything's here: <link>. Let me know if you have any questions."
- **"When's the next masterclass?"** → "Next one is <date>! Save your spot here: <link>."
- **"Where can I get the hat?"** → "Join for the year and it's yours, free: <link> 🧢"
- **"Where do I start?"** → "Join our free Rep of the Day group: one small rep a day. <link>"
- **"I can't find the replay / workbook"** → "Sorry about that! I'm looping in our team now — we'll get you sorted today." → tag Michelle (and Logan if it's an app issue).

## Escalate to Michelle

- Anything about payment, refunds, or account access
- Negative/critical comments that need a careful reply
- Press, collaboration, or partnership requests
- Anything you're unsure about
