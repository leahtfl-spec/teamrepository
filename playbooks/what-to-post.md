# What to Post

## Content pillars

Every post should fit one of these. Aim for a mix across the week. *(Percentages are starting assumptions; adjust them to what performs.)*

The **Rep of the Day** runs separately in the Facebook group. See [Rep of the Day](community-rep-of-the-day.md).

| Pillar | Goal | Examples | Share of posts |
|--------|------|----------|----------------|
| **Masterclass clips** | Show the value of what we teach | 30–60 sec teaching moments, "aha" quotes | ~30% |
| **Education** | Give value for free, build trust | Tips, carousels, myths vs. facts | ~25% |
| **Community & wins** | Social proof | Member testimonials, results, shout-outs | ~15% |
| **Behind the scenes** | Make us human & relatable | Team in the gear, Recording Block, how we build | ~15% |
| **Promo / offers** | Drive sign-ups | Masterclass invites, Founding 1,000 hat offer, Gift a Year | ~15% |

## Getting the most out of one masterclass

One masterclass should feed **at least 2 weeks** of content:

- 5–8 short clips (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)
- 2–3 quote graphics
- 1 carousel summarizing the key lessons
- 1 "if you missed it, it's in the membership" promo post
- 1 highlight reel

## Quality checklist (Clint, before publishing)

- [ ] Hook in the first 2 seconds
- [ ] Captions on every video
- [ ] On-brand fonts, colors, logo
- [ ] **One** clear call to action, usually "join the free Rep of the Day group"
- [ ] Founder and team in the gear where possible
- [ ] Caption written + hashtags
- [ ] Logged in the content calendar
- [ ] Offer or pricing posts only: Abra has signed off

## Status values (content calendar)

`planned` → `in edit` → `scheduled` → `posted` (offer/pricing posts add `approved` before `scheduled`)
