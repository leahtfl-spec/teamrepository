# The Becoming You Show (podcast)

**The podcast is how people find us.** Every episode should give real value and end with **one** call to action.

**Host:** Founder. **Producer:** Clint. **Growth and guests:** Abra. **Member Spotlight segment:** Michelle.

## Cadence

- **Weekly** episode, released the same day each week (suggested: Thursday).
- Recorded in the founder's weekly **Recording Block**, 2 weeks ahead.

## Every episode includes

- [ ] One clear call to action, rotated by phase:
  - Default: "Join the free Rep of the Day group" (link)
  - December: "Gift the practice to someone you love"
  - January: "Join the Founding 1,000 and get your hat"
- [ ] The founder wearing the gear on video (if the episode is filmed)
- [ ] 3–5 short clips cut for social (Clint)
- [ ] Full episode on YouTube (Clint)
- [ ] Audio also uploaded to the app as a member audio drop where it fits (Logan)
- [ ] Download numbers recorded in the scorecard (Clint)

## Episode types (rotate)

| Type | How often | Who |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Founder solo teaching | 2× per month | Founder |
| Guest conversation | 1× per month | Founder + guest (booked by Abra) |
| **Member Spotlight**: a member's story of becoming | 1× per month | Michelle hosts or co-hosts |
| Behind Inner Board: the team, the gear, how we build | Quarterly | Founder + team |

## Growth (Abra)

- Pitch the founder to **2–4 other podcasts a month**. This is the biggest reach lever.
- Trade guest spots with similar shows.
- Ask listeners to share one episode with one friend.
