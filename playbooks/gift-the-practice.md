# Gift the Practice: Merch & Promotions

Gear does three jobs:
1. **It gives people a reason to commit now:** a free hat with an annual plan.
2. **It makes us recognizable:** the team wears it on camera and members wear it in their photos.
3. **It makes members feel they belong:** a member in an Inner Board hat is a walking invitation.

**Offer owner:** Abra. **Design:** Clint. **Fulfillment:** Michelle. **Gift codes and annual flag:** Logan.

## The three programs

### 1. Founding 1,000: join annually, get a free hat
- Anyone who buys (or upgrades to) an **annual** plan gets an Inner Board hat.
- Framed as **"Founding Member"**, limited to the first 1,000 annual members.
- **Fine print:** one per member; shipping region (e.g. US only?); ships within X weeks; while supplies last.

### 2. Gift a Year (holiday season)
- Buy a year of the practice for someone, and they receive a welcome hat and a card.
- **Requires:** gift codes in the app or payment system. Logan confirms in Phase 0.

### 3. Streak rewards
- 100 Rep of the Day reps earns an Inner Board sweatshirt.
- Tracked manually at first (members post "Day 100").

## The team wears it

- Founder: in every video, podcast recording and live session.
- Abra, Michelle and Clint: in their own on-camera moments and team content.
- Clint runs one team shoot in the gear. That content feeds December and January.

## Check the economics before launching (Abra)

| Item | Fill in |
|------|---------|
| Annual price | $ |
| Hat cost (unit + embroidery) | $ |
| Shipping + packaging per hat | $ |
| **Total cost per hat** | $ |
| **Hat as % of annual price** | aim for under ~15% |

**Rule:** if the hat costs more than about 15% of the annual price, raise the price, find a cheaper vendor, or limit the offer to the first N members.

## Sourcing

| Option | Good | Bad | Use for |
|--------|------|-----|---------|
| **Bulk order** (local embroiderer or bulk vendor) | Cheapest per unit; you can hand them out and shoot content | Upfront cash; you hold inventory | Hats for the team and the first wave (start with about 100–150) |
| **Print-on-demand** (made per order) | No inventory or upfront cost | Higher unit cost; slower; less control over quality | Sweatshirts; a store where members buy their own |

## Fulfillment flow (Michelle)

1. Logan flags new annual and gift signups weekly.
2. Michelle sends a short address form.
3. Ship hats in one weekly batch.
4. Record "hats owed" and "hats shipped" in the scorecard.
5. Members post a photo in the hat, and Clint reposts it.
