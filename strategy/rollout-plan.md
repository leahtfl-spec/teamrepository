# Rollout Plan

A phased plan built around the calendar we're actually in: **gift season (December) and New Year (January)** are the two best moments of the year for a practice called *Becoming You*.

> **Timing warning:** custom embroidered hats usually take **2–4 weeks** to produce and ship. To have hats in hand for December, **order by about Nov 20**. If that isn't possible, run Gift the Practice as "hat ships in January" and say so up front.

---
