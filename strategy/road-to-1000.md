## The funnel math (starting assumptions; replace with real numbers)

| Step | Assumption | Per month to hit ~84 new paid |
|------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| New free group members | — | **~1,050** |
| Group → masterclass registrants | 40% | ~420 |
| Registrants who show up | 50% | ~210 |
| Attendees who join the app | 25% | ~53 |
| Direct joins (podcast, socials, referrals, gifts) | — | ~31 |
| **Total new paid** | | **~84** |

**What the math tells you:** the growth constraint is **about 1,000 new people a month entering the free group**. That has to come from:
- **The podcast,** with one call to action each episode ("Join the free Rep of the Day group").
- **The founder appearing on other podcasts**: 2–4 guest spots a month is the highest-leverage reach for a lean team.
- **Clips** that send people to the group, not just likes.
- **Members inviting people**: gift a year, plus "bring a friend to the 30-day challenge".

**First action (Abra, this week):** write down where we actually are right now: paid members, annual vs. monthly, email list size, podcast downloads per episode, social followers. You can't manage a gap you haven't measured.
