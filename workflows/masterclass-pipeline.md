# Masterclass Pipeline

How one masterclass becomes: a replay in the app, membership assets, and a week+ of social content.

**Owner of the whole pipeline: Michelle.** Each step has one person responsible.
To kick one off: **Issues → New issue → Masterclass** (it creates this checklist for you).

---

## Timeline

`Day 0` = the day of the masterclass.

| When | Step | Owner | Handoff to |
|------|------|-------|-----------|
| Day −7 | Confirm date, topic, title, and promo plan | Michelle | Abra (approve), Clint |
| Day −7 | Draft workbook outline | Michelle | — |
| Day −5 → −1 | Promo posts go out (announcement, reminder, "tomorrow", "today") | Clint | — |
| Day −1 | Tech check: recording software, audio, backup recording | Michelle + Clint | — |
| **Day 0** | **Run masterclass; record (with a backup)** | Michelle | — |
| Day 0 | Clint captures behind-the-scenes / talking-head footage | Clint | — |
| Day +1 | Raw recording uploaded to shared folder; Clint & Logan notified | Michelle | Clint, Logan |
| Day +2 | Clean replay edit (trim start/end, fix audio) | Clint | Logan |
| Day +2 | Audio drop exported (audio-only version) | Clint | Logan |
| Day +3 | Workbook finalized | Michelle | Logan |
| Day +3 | Replay, audio drop, workbook, slides **live in the app** | Logan | Michelle |
| Day +3 | Michelle checks the app on phone + desktop; signs off | Michelle | Abra |
| Day +3 → +14 | Clips posted from the masterclass (see calendar) | Clint | — |

> Adjust the days to what works for you — the point is that **every step has a date and a name**.

---

## Shared folder structure

Keep every masterclass in the same layout so nobody has to ask where something is:

```
Masterclasses/
  2026-11-15 – <Masterclass Title>/
    01-raw/          ← Michelle drops raw recording here
    02-replay/       ← Clint's cleaned replay
    03-audio/        ← Audio drop (mp3)
    04-workbook/     ← Workbook (PDF)
    05-slides/       ← Slides & extra resources
    06-clips/        ← Clint's short-form clips & graphics
```

---

## Handoff rules

1. **Every handoff is announced** — comment on the masterclass issue and tag the next person (e.g. "@Logan replay is in 02-replay").
2. **Nothing goes in the app until Michelle has signed off** on the file.
3. **Nothing goes on social until Michelle has approved** the clip.
4. If a step is going to be late, say so **before** the due date, on the issue.
