# Weekly Cadence

The week is built around one thing: **the founder's Recording Block**. Everything else feeds it or distributes it.
Specific posts and dates live in the [Content Calendar](content-calendar.csv). Numbers live in the [Scorecard](../strategy/scorecard.csv).

## Every week

| Day | Founder | Abra (Chief of Staff & Growth) | Michelle (COO & Member Experience) | Clint (Creative Director) | Logan (App) |
|-----|---------|------|----------|-------|-------|
| **Mon** | 15 min at the leadership meeting (optional) | **Runs the leadership meeting** (30 min, from the scorecard) | Leadership meeting; group welcome post | Leadership meeting; plan the Recording Block shot list | Fix any open app issues |
| **Tue** | **Recording Block** (about half a day): podcast + 7 Reps + promo videos | Guards the founder's calendar; podcast pitches | Masterclass prep / onboarding work | **Directs and shoots the Recording Block** | — |
| **Wed** | — | Guest bookings; offers and promotions | Community; member support | Edit podcast + Reps | Upload new assets |
| **Thu** | — | Partnerships; email list | Check in with Logan; ship hats | **Podcast episode releases**; post clips; schedule Reps 2 weeks out | Upload Reps / audio to the app |
| **Fri** | **Live session in the group** (15–20 min) | **Update the scorecard** | **Wins Thread** + member spotlight; send group numbers to Abra | Social numbers + podcast downloads to Abra | App numbers to Abra |
| **Sat/Sun** | — | — | Light group check (15 min/day) | Scheduled posts go out | — |

## Daily non-negotiables

- **Rep of the Day** goes out at 6am (scheduled by Clint).
- **Michelle:** two 15-minute passes through the group (replies, welcomes, moderation).
- **Clint:** two passes through comments and messages on our social accounts.
