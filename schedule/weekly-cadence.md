# Weekly Cadence

The recurring rhythm. Specific posts and dates live in [`content-calendar.csv`](content-calendar.csv).

## Every week

| Day | Abra | Michelle | Clint | Logan |
|-----|------|----------|-------|-------|
| **Mon** | **Weekly review** with Michelle (30 min): last week's results, this week's priorities | Weekly review; update content calendar 2 weeks out; assign the week's issues | Plan the week's posts; batch-edit clips | Check app for any open fixes |
| **Tue** | — | Approve Clint's clips for Tue–Thu | Post + engage (see playbook) | Upload any pending assets |
| **Wed** | — | 1:1 / training with Clint (20 min) | Post + engage | — |
| **Thu** | — | Check in with Logan on uploads | Post + engage; batch-edit next week's clips | Upload any pending assets |
| **Fri** | Quick check: anything blocked? | Approve weekend posts; send Abra a status update | Schedule weekend posts; weekly stats snapshot | Weekly app check (everything plays?) |
| **Sat/Sun** | — | — | Scheduled posts go out; light engagement check (15 min/day) | — |

## Daily non-negotiables

- **Clint:** respond to comments/DMs at least twice a day (see [`how-to-engage.md`](../playbooks/how-to-engage.md)).
- **Michelle:** check open issues once a day — anything overdue gets a comment.

## Monthly

| When | What | Who |
|------|------|-----|
| First Monday | Set the month's priorities, masterclass dates, launches | Abra + Michelle |
| First Monday | Fill content calendar for the month | Michelle + Clint |
| Last Friday | Monthly results: follower growth, engagement, members, app usage | Clint (social stats) + Logan (app stats) → Michelle → Abra |
